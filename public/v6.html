4. Research safeguards

  • Chronological splits: 55% training, 15% probability calibration, 15% threshold selection, 15% untouched holdout.
  • Boundary purge prevents expiry labels from crossing between data partitions.
  • Signals are limited to one trade per expiry window (1, 2 or 5 minutes) to prevent overlapping positions while allowing the requested frequency to be tested.
  • Threshold selection searches a finer confidence grid. A pass requires at least 100 validation trades AND 100 unseen holdout trades, target accuracy, positive net units and requested frequency. If any condition fails, the result is NO EDGE.
  • Historical Dukascopy bid candles are not the broker's exact binary settlement quotes. This is a research proxy, not proof of executable profitability.
