The experts are independently regularized logistic models. Their blended output is calibrated on a chronological calibration slice. The threshold is chosen only on the validation slice; the holdout is not used to tune the model.

5. Validation safeguards

  • Chronological partitions: 60% training, 15% calibration, 10% validation, 15% untouched holdout.
  • Expiry-boundary purge prevents labels from crossing between partitions.
  • Entry is the next M1 candle open; expiry is the selected expiry candle close.
  • Only continuous minute sequences are eligible; gaps, invalid OHLC rows and ties are excluded.
  • Trades cannot overlap: the cooldown is at least the selected expiry.
  • A PASS requires at least 100 validation and 100 holdout trades, 75% target accuracy, positive net units and 5–10 trades per active hour in both validation and holdout.
  • If no validation threshold meets the requirements, the model is not tuned against the holdout. It reports NO EDGE.
Dukascopy bid candles are a historical proxy, not the exact binary-platform settlement feed. Payout is an assumption. Historical success cannot guarantee future results.
