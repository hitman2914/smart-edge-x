Signal integrity

The backtest uses closed signal candles and the next minute open as entry. The first 70% of the timeline is the design segment; the final 30% is a chronological holdout. Ties are excluded. Dukascopy bid candles are only a proxy for the binary platform settlement feed.

Evidence score is a rule-agreement score, NOT a probability of winning. A profitable historical result is not a guarantee of future results.
