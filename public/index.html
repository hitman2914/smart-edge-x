Signal integrity

The backtest uses only closed candles and the next candle close as the entry reference to reduce look-ahead bias. A trade outcome is determined by comparing the expiry candle close with the entry price. Ties are excluded.

Evidence score is a rule-agreement score, NOT a probability of winning. A profitable historical result is not a guarantee of future results.
